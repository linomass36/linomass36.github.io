Most of these are in the hub's Settings tab. After changing card settings here, open Year 2 > Card plan and press **Re-plan from today**.

**ceiling**: the most new cards a normal day can hold, per deck. The plan uses `reserve` of it (85%: about 21 AnKing cards a day); the rest is spare capacity for weak days. Anki's preset is set to the ceiling, so a phone session never shows more.

**crunch_ceiling**: the ceiling in Crunch weeks (partials, the special senses week). Rest days are 0; Push weeks (breaks) are 1.5 x the ceiling.

**reserve**: the share of each day the plan fills (0.85). Lower it for more slack, raise it to finish units sooner.

**buffer_days**: the cards planned for the next days that are unsuspended in advance, so a phone session or a good day can go ahead.

**light_day_factor**: what a Light day keeps (0.5 = half).

**core_tiers**: AnKing yield tiers on the schedule, per track (0 = no yield tag, 1 = HighYield). **extra / extra_tiers**: lower tiers planned only on days with room.

**course_tracks**: planned before the Step weeks.

**confirm_tracks**: tracks that wait for your OK before their cards are released, e.g. ["physio"]. Empty: everything releases on its date.

**apply_limits**: set each day's new-card number on the deck (today only).

**set_queue_order**: make the AnKing and SecondLook presets show new cards in plan order.

**qid_cap_per_question**: AnKing cards unsuspended per missed UWorld question.

**horizon**: the last day the plan schedules (end of July 2027).

**decks**: the exact names of your AnKing and SecondLook decks.

**tolerance_days**: a unit that would finish up to this many days after its first-plan date is caught up with the spare capacity; beyond that the plan moves and tells you.

**show_panel**: show the hub on the deck screen.
