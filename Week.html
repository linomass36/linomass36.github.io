The calendar this site reads is read-only, so hold a date or an evening with friends here instead and the week plans round it: the training moves for it exactly as it moves for a shift, the Grind board lays the day out round it, and a re-pull cannot delete it.

Why this is dated
The Grind board keys its sessions week|slot — 3|mon — which says what was done but not when. These are stored by date, so a session done on the 3rd is a fact about the 3rd, and trained becomes a column the correlations can read. The two are joined now rather than merely both existing: ticking a session here marks it on the board and on the Life Log for the same day, and the board lays its week out on the days this page found for it.
