The rules

What trips a flag
R1Any quiz or test under 70%: error card for every miss and name the cause before the next class.
R2Two results in a row under 80% in one subject: the method isn't working. Change one thing.
R3The same cause in two of your last five results: fix the cause everywhere, not one subject.
R4Error cards not made two days after a result.
R5A class within two days with fewer than two prep items ticked.
R6Lab or seminar passed without cards made within 24 hours.
R7Two classes in 14 days in one subject with under two prep items: the week isn't giving that subject time.
R8Recall under 80% in a subject: one week is a warning, two weeks means nothing new goes live until it's read and blank-paged.
R9Anki over 150 real min/day for a week: cut new cards in the subject whose next test is furthest away.
R10Last week's Anki row missing by Tuesday: send the export.
R11You lead the session tomorrow or today and the prompt card and key aren't written.
R12A stacked week is 21, 14 or 7 days out, under way, or just over: the protocol's countdown step for it.
R13A practice set under 80% this week: 70–79 means set 2 and a re-read, under 70 a re-read and a fresh set. Two in a row under 70 is a method review.
R14A new-card cap broken: over 100 new anatomy cards on one day, or AnKing over 60 min a day for the week.
