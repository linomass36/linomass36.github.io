## Standing rules (every year)

**Career**
- One gate: US GS 2031. Four levers: Step 2 CK, months of US clinical time, American letters from people who watched you work, research that is surgery / heart / chest.
- I-6 kill-switch, spring 2030: you need most of — elite Step 2, US CT letter from more than two weeks, CT paper or STS/AATS-level abstract, CT or high-volume academic surgery sub-I, a CT surgeon who will email a PD. Missing two or more → I-6 off, GS only.
- Unmatched branch is written by **February 2031**: named US surgical research-year labs, mentors, funding. Not invented in March.
- MUL American track = cores at affiliates (Brooklyn / One Brooklyn Health and other listed sites). Letters from those months count. They are not automatically CT. A CT month is a separate ask every year you are in the US.
- AATS and similar: confirm UMLub eligibility once. If no, never build a summer around them.

**Money**
- Draw federal loans to the annual cap (~$50k) until the lifetime cap (~$200k). Tuition is not a July job.
- Gap above federal (~$170k in the old model): **one written number by June 2027** — savings you will actually have + NYC living you will actually pay + MUL/hospital aid + private principal cap if anything remains. June-savings-story and August-private-story cannot both stay live.
- RAP + PSLF if you work academic/nonprofit. Payments on time and in full. Joint filing can pull a spouse’s AGI. Forgiveness can be taxable. This is policy, not a vow you make to a girlfriend.
- Two pots: **Nest** (household buffer) and **Move/interview** (applications, flights, deposits). Do not loot the nest in Year 6.
- Nest targets: $1–3k after summer 2027; $8–15k when Y4 starts; still in that band at match after the move line has its own money.
- No motorcycle unless two Phoenix summers are on paper. Sell before US clinical years.

**Spouse**
- Love language you already have: letters, flowers, attention, gifts you can afford. Not itineraries.
- “Fun doesn’t stop” = she is inside the week, not parked until 2039. A walk, a meal, a call that is not a lecture.
- Visit rule: trips split or they stop. You do not recreate 5:1.
- If four days apart already wrecked you, you do not sign a months-apart structure unless a visit cadence you both fund is real. Calls help. They are not the visit.
- NYC together = American track **and** her work/school/research actually in that city. Two gates. Not a 2026 promise.
- Timeline spoken early: 2031 match, long training, moves, ugly months, attending ~2039. Marry on the trajectory.
- She is not the vault. Five minutes on the file, then her life.
- Standing contact that survives exams. Cancel → reschedule before hangup.
- Do not wait to date until the nest or the match. Do not start LD you already know you cannot stand.

**Presence under the weight**
- Two rooms. School in one. Her in the other. Close the door.
- Year 2 this term is only: American-track email, anatomy + cards, abstract out, one CT ask. The rest of this document is not this week.
