School year in Poland: **October → start of July** (nine full school months: Oct–Jun). July–August are summer.
Start after Arizona: **5 000 zł**.
Studying is first. Nothing below is allowed to steal a failing block.

## Monthly zł (school months)

Out 3 000 (rent 1 800, food 800, extra 400).
In guaranteed 2 200 (allowance 1 200, research 1 000 for **30 h**).
Hole **−800** before scholarship, tutoring, GF, paper.
Scholarship 700 Oct–Jun, **2 800 in January**, 700 Feb–Jun.
GF cheap: ~80–90 zł/month. Optional; tables below omit it unless noted (~−80 extra).
Paper on schedule (cannot pause for lack of materials). First Poland lot **Nov ~2 200 zł**. Next lots ~every 5–6 months (~2 000). No scanner.
Ring: first sweep **Oct 2028** (a year later than the old Sep 2027 start) — after this window, in NYC. **0 in this file.**
Cushion target 5 000. Paper still buys on schedule, so **income must rise**. Cushion will break unless tutoring or family/federal fills.

Research wage implied: 1 000 / 30 h ≈ **33 zł/h**.
Local tutoring: **50 zł/h**.
US tutoring: **$20/h ≈ 75 zł/h**.

## Hours in a school month (720 h clock, ~510 awake)

| Block | Hours | Rank |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep | 210 (7 h × 30) | hard floor |
| Studying | 160–200 (40–50 h/week) | **#1, no comparison** |
| Paid research | 30 | kept; it is the 1 000 zł |
| Hospital assistant | **cap 30 / plan 15** | unpaid letter; average 15 unless a writing week needs more |
| Bible manuscript | 20–30 (45–60 min most days) | craft; do not skip materials, do skip extra flourishes |
| Tutoring | 0–32 | income lever; 32 h is 8 h/week |
| Life / GF train / clinic transit | the rest | |

**Rule:** if a block score is in danger, cut hospital first (toward 0), then tutoring hours, then Bible *time* (not the paper lot already ordered). Never cut study for 50 zł.

Hospital cap **30 h**. Default **15 h** (about one shift-and-a-half a week). Use up to 30 only in a week that produces the December letter. Year 27/28 spring: hospital → 0 if Step 1 needs the slot. 15 h unpaid ≈ the cash of 5 h local tutoring you did not sell.

Bible at 20–30 h plus a Nov paper lot is consistent: money for materials, not a second 40 h craft week.

## Time vs money (pick one honest week)

Awake hours ~510. Study 180 + research 30 + sleep is already sacred. Left for hospital + Bible + tutoring + life ≈ **100–120 h**.

| Package | Hospital | Bible | Tutoring | Fits under study-first? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Letter + book, broke | 15–30 | 25 | 0 | yes, cash dies |
| Paper + floor | 15 | 25 | 12–16 local | yes |
| Ticket, local rate | 15 | 20 | 24–32 local | tight; watch sleep |
| Ticket, USD rate | 15 | 20 | 16–20 US | yes if clients exist |
| Max cash | 15 | 20 | 32 US | only if study scores hold |
| Everything maxed | 30 | 30 | 32 | **no** |

Default week that can buy paper *and* not fail:

- Study first.  
- Research 30 h.  
- Hospital **15 h** average, never above **30**.  
- Bible **~20–25 h**, lot when due.  
- Tutoring **16–20 h US or 24–32 h local** if the summer is self-funded.  
- 32 h tutoring is **8 h/week**. Stacked on 15 hospital + 30 research that is already 77 h of non-study work per month before the book. That is survivable. 30 + 32 + 30 Bible is not.

Year 27/28 spring: hospital → 0 if Step 1 needs the hours. Tutoring may drop in the last 8–10 weeks before the exam. Bank cash before that cut.

## Two-year sentence

October–June is nine months of −800 plus two paper lots. Scholarship only patches after January. Bare bones ends the first school year at about **zero or red** and cannot fly. **12–16 hours a month of tutoring**, preferably USD, is what makes “paper on schedule + not destitute” true. Hospital is a letter, priced in hours you could have tutored. Study stays first; if the week is failing, you drop hospital and tutoring hours, not the exam, and you still buy the paper that is already due.
