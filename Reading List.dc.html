One book, one note — kept in step both ways
Every book becomes a Markdown note in {{ obs.folder }}: status, dates, where you are, your timestamped notes and the reflection, all in the frontmatter and body. Write in Obsidian or write here — a sync merges both sides, and a note you delete on one side stays deleted.
